ORB Garment Studio v0.9.51 — Faster Neutral Lighting

INSTALL OVER v0.9.50
Replace index.html and merge assets, replacing matching files. Keep the other
files. Refresh your local preview and confirm v0.9.51 in Help.
Neutral remains the default, first in the toolbar, at a 100% baseline.

WHAT CHANGED
- Removed the nine-shadow-light rig responsible for the slowdown.
- Neutral now uses one 1024 px shadow map, the existing soft shadow filter,
  and the existing environment, fill and rim illumination.
- Main-light strength compensates for replacing the distributed source with
  one light, keeping brightness and color close to the approved C treatment.
- No additional HDRI downloads, baked garment textures, controls or menus.
- The viewer and Neutral mockup exports share this same cheaper setup.
- Other presets retain their previous shadow-map budgets and soft filter.
- Static shadow maps remain cached. Motion and camera changes update one map
  for Neutral instead of nine. Replaced shadow targets are disposed when
  switching resolutions.

VISUAL TRADEOFF
This is a close approximation of C, not a pixel-identical area-light render.
Fine shadow edges can differ. Compared white, gray, tan and black during the
lighting experiments, and inspected the tee and hoodie in final front/back
exports. The existing soft filter gave cleaner results than a much wider
filter kernel and avoids additional shader variants.
Fabric textures, normal maps, artwork and garment geometry remain unchanged.

PERFORMANCE CHECK
Controlled men's tee test at 360 x 440 px, with a shadow update forced on every
frame and four measured frames after warm-up:
- Shadow maps: 9 -> 1.
- Total draw calls, including shadows: 40 -> 8 (80% fewer).
- Rendered triangles per frame: 3,651,180 -> 730,236.
- Median software-WebGL render time: 2,583 ms -> 806 ms (about 3.2x faster).
These software-renderer timings establish a relative improvement in this test;
they do not predict FPS on your workstation. The map count, draw count and
shader simplification also remove the extra work from the interactive viewer.
