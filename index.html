Artwork & layers
Library
Drop several images into the library to reuse them across layers.
Move
Move the selected layer to a standard position. Left and right refer to the wearer.
Duplicate
Make a copy you can edit independently.
Layer order
Drag the grip or use the arrow buttons. Higher layers appear on top.
Rename & undo
Use the pencil to rename. Undo and Redo restore design edits.
Color & effects
Original
Keep the uploaded colors and transparency.
Tint
Color the lighter areas while retaining dark shading.
Solid
Print one ink color. Dark pixels reveal the fabric; existing transparency is preserved.
Solid settings
Cutoff removes dark detail. Lower Softness builds heavier ink; higher Softness keeps gentler transitions. Invert mask prints dark artwork instead.
Color swatches
Click a swatch to edit or use the eyedropper to sample. Session colors are shared.
Glow & UV
Glow shows in darkness and shadow. UV reactive responds to Black light. Glossiness affects all layers.
Preview & lighting
Camera & wind
Choose a camera angle or wind preset above the preview. Detail opens artwork, neck, hem, shoulder-blade and wrist close-ups. Present hides the interface.
Environment
Choose lighting, background, and grid. Type any higher intensity value to expand the slider, or drag to its end and release to extend it. Black light previews UV effects.
City night
Dim streetlight with passing traffic. Choose Off, Subtle, or Active, and pause to hold a lighting moment. Export All holds the lights still across views.
Fix lights in scene
Keep lights stationary as you rotate the garment.
Theme
Change the interface appearance. Match fabric to theme also switches garment color.
Saving & exporting
Save design
Download an editable .orb file. Reopen with Open or drag it into the studio.
Save Image
Export the current view as a PNG.
Export All
Package framed views, treated artwork and originals in one ZIP, with an optional presentation sheet and editable design.
Browser memory
Your design and library stay in this browser. Save a design file to take them elsewhere or keep a backup.
Using your own 3D model
Upload
Use a GLB with embedded textures and non-overlapping UVs.
Set position
Select a layer, choose Set position, then tap the model.
Orientation
Turn around corrects the model direction. Restore selection returns to the built-in garment.
