Quick guide

ORB Garment Studio

  1. 01

    Add artwork

    Drop an image onto the workspace and choose a placement. On mobile, tap Add artwork. Left and right refer to the wearer.

  2. 02

    Arrange your layers

    Select a graphic to open its controls directly below the row. Add multiple graphics to any placement. Drag the grip to reorder them; higher layers appear on top. On touch or keyboard, you can also use ↑ ↓. Use × to remove one graphic. Undo restores it.

  3. 03

    Color and finish

    Original is the default for uploads and preserves image colors, backgrounds, and transparency. The starting ORB graphics use Solid with automatic shirt contrast until you choose an ink color. Tint changes the color family while preserving light/dark detail and transparency. Solid uses one flat color, preserving soft edges and extracting simple light or dark backgrounds. Click a mode label to activate it, its swatch to edit color, or the eyedropper to sample; each layer remembers separate Tint and Solid colors. Choose garment colors and print glossiness in the sidebar; Fit visible artwork trims transparent margins. Use the pencil or double-click a layer name to rename it. Enter saves, Escape cancels; the original filename appears while editing.

  4. 04

    Preview and export

    Drag to rotate; scroll or pinch to zoom. Use camera and wind presets above. In Environment, choose Studio, Outdoor day, City night, or Black light; adjust light intensity and night colors there. Lighting follows your view by default. Check Fix lights in scene to keep the rig stationary while you orbit. Garment self-shadows add depth to overlapping cloth and can be disabled for performance. The middle theme button follows your system’s light or dark appearance. Theme changes affect the interface and background while preserving garment colors and lighting. Enable Match fabric to theme beside the fabric colors for automatic light/dark fabric switching. Choosing a fabric color turns that option off. Environment also controls the background and grid. In Black light, dark fabric retains its matte reflections and pale fabric gains a small UV-dependent glow. Type higher intensity values to expand its slider; Black light remembers its own intensity separately from other presets. The preview switches to a black background and faint grid; leaving Black light restores your previous background and grid colors. Switching garments preserves your viewing angle and zoom. In Artwork, Glossiness applies to all layers. Each expanded artwork layer has Glow in the dark and UV reactive controls; enable either to reveal its intensity slider. Glow becomes visible in darkness and shadow; UV reactivity responds to the Black light preset. Emission includes a subtle local color spill onto surrounding fabric. UV has a dim ambient response in the Black light preset. Zoom out from a closeup, or press Done in its artwork controls, to return to the garment center. Present hides the interface; Save Image exports the current view.

Using your own 3D model

Choose one of the four garments at the top of the sidebar. Artwork uses each garment’s own UV panels. You can also upload a GLB with embedded textures and non-overlapping UVs. Select an artwork row, choose Set position, then tap the model. Turn around corrects its orientation; Restore selection returns to the built-in garment.
